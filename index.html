Nutrition & Fuel
▾
🎯
Daily Protein
180g/day (~2.0g/kg at 194 lb). Protects muscle while cutting. Spread across 5–6 meals.
📉
Calories (Cut)
~2,300–2,450 kcal/day (~400 below TDEE) → ~1–1.3 lb/week. Adjust off the weekly-average scale.
🌾
Fiber
Daily psyllium husk (5–10g) with a full glass of water. Best soluble fiber for LDL. Aim 30–40g total.
🫒
Saturated Fat
Keep it modest. Swap butter/fatty meat for olive oil, nuts, avocado, fish. Helps LDL + Lp(a) profile.
🐟
Omega-3
Fatty fish 2–3×/week (salmon, sardines, mackerel). Walnuts, chia, flax.
🍷
Alcohol
Occasional, not routine. Raises triglycerides, hurts sleep + BP.
🥛
Post-Training
Whey shake within 30–60 min if protein target is short.
😴
Sleep
7–9h. Protects muscle, appetite control, and lipids on a cut.
